# Writing style

Injected into every research run. Edit it to change how pages read.

- Lead each section with the plain-language answer, then the detail.
- Short paragraphs. One idea per paragraph.
- Define a term the first time it appears.
- Prefer concrete numbers, names and dates over adjectives, and say when a number comes from an old or narrow test.
- Quote a source only when the exact wording matters; otherwise paraphrase and cite.
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless").
- When sources disagree, say who claims what instead of picking a side silently.
